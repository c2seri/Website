Privacy Policy

Compliant with Information Technology Act, 2000 • DPDP Act, 2023 • TRAI DLT Guidelines

At C2S — College to Service (“C2S”, “we”, “our”), we take your privacy and personal data seriously. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you access c2seri.com or visit our offline centre at NH-16 Patrapada, Bhubaneswar.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact Details: Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Number, Residential City/Address.
  • Academic Profile: Target examinations (e.g., OSSC CGL, Bank PO, SSC GD), chosen batch timings, and library shift preferences.
  • Test & Performance Data: CBT mock test attempts, scores, question-level analytics, rank percentiles, and exam violation records.
  • Technical Analytics: Device type, browser user agent, IP address, and page interaction metrics captured anonymously via Google Tag (gtag.js G-HJ59JX1P5N).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process your admission inquiries, classroom enrollments, and library desk reservations.
  • To issue secure One-Time Passwords (OTPs) for account access and CBT test validation.
  • To generate All-India and Odisha State comparative scorecards and performance rank metrics.
  • To transmit official recruitment notifications, exam date releases, syllabus changes, and batch timetable notifications.

3. Direct Communication Consent (SMS, WhatsApp, RCS & Calls)

Under TRAI DLT regulations and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023, by checking the consent box on our contact/registration forms, you provide explicit consent to receive service updates, transactional OTPs, and educational announcements via SMS, WhatsApp, RCS, and automated phone calls from C2S. You may revoke promotional communication consent at any time by emailing us at contact@c2seri.com.

Our Absolute Promise: C2S never sells, rents, leases, or trades student contact numbers, emails, or academic records to third-party telemarketers, credit card issuers, or commercial aggregators. Your information stays strictly within C2S.

4. Secure Cloud Data Storage & Payments

Student credentials and database records are securely hosted on Supabase South Asia (ap-south-1 Mumbai) cloud infrastructure featuring row-level security and SSL encryption. Online fee payments are processed via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (Cashfree). C2S does not store or process complete credit card numbers, CVV codes, or net banking passwords.
