OFFICIAL C2S CODE OF CONDUCT

LIBRARY USE RULES & INSTRUCTIONS

NH-16 Patrapada Centre, Bhubaneswar • "Your Selection, Our Mission"

  1. Maintain complete silence inside the library. Avoid loud conversations and unnecessary phone calls.
  2. ID proof and Photo are mandatory to avail all facilities from the library.
  3. Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode; no other mode is allowed.
  4. No phone calls are allowed inside the reading area.
  5. Keep your seat and surroundings clean. Keep the premises clean. Use dustbins for waste disposal.
  6. Eating, smoking, and chewing tobacco are strictly prohibited inside library.
  7. Smoking, vaping, and the use of tobacco products are strictly prohibited anywhere inside the building. Any violation may lead to immediate cancellation of library membership.
  8. Drinking water is allowed only in designated areas.
  9. Use library furniture and books carefully.
  10. Do not damage, write on, or tear books or furniture.
  11. Maintain your allotted seat only.
  12. Follow your registered shift timing.
  13. Entry is allowed only after attendance/registration.
  14. Visitors are not allowed without permission.
  15. Do not disturb other students.
  16. Switch off lights and fans if instructed while leaving.
  17. Bags should be kept in the designated area, if applicable.
  18. The entire building including library is under 24×7 CCTV surveillance for the safety and security of all students and staff.
  19. Management reserves the right to cancel membership for misconduct.
  20. Follow all instructions given by the library staff.
  21. Keep your ID card with you and produce it when asked.
  22. Any misconduct, indiscipline, or nuisance will result in immediate termination of library membership without refund.
  23. Management reserves the right to refuse or cancel admission in case of violation of library rules.
  24. Help maintain a peaceful and disciplined study environment.
