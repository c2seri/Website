Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear, fair, and transparent fee policies for Classroom Batches, Library, and Test Series.

At C2S — College to Service, we strive to deliver the highest quality education, smart classroom teaching, and study library facilities. We believe in complete transparency regarding fees, cancellations, and refunds.

Quick Summary: Classroom batches have a 7-day refund window (less registration/material costs). 24h Study Library registration fee ($200) is non-refundable; shift fee refunds are pro-rata before shift start. Digital CBT mock test packages and PDF e-books are non-refundable once accessed. Double charges are 100% refunded.

1. Offline Classroom Coaching Batches (Patrapada Centre)

  • Trial / Demo Classes: Aspirants are encouraged to attend designated demo/trial classes prior to formal fee payment to satisfy themselves with teaching quality and faculty pedagogy.
  • Withdrawal Within 7 Days: If an enrolled student wishes to cancel their admission within 7 calendar days from the official batch commencement date, tuition fees paid will be refunded after deducting a nominal administrative registration fee ($500) and the cost of printed books/study kit if already issued.
  • After 7 Days: No refund requests or fee adjustments will be entertained after 7 calendar days from the batch start date, or once a student has attended more than 4 sessions.

2. 24-Hour Premium Study Library Memberships

  • One-Time Registration Fee: The initial registration fee of $200 is non-refundable and non-transferable under all circumstances, as it covers biometric profile generation and seat administrative setup.
  • Shift Membership Cancellation: If a student cancels their shift reservation before the shift start date or within the first 3 days due to verifiable medical/relocation emergency, a pro-rata refund for the unutilized period will be granted. No refund is applicable after 3 days of seat utilization.

3. Online CBT Mock Tests, Test Passes & Digital Study Notes

  • Because digital products (CBT mock test engine access, detailed solutions, and downloadable PDF current affairs handbooks) are delivered immediately upon account activation, fees paid for online test series and e-books are strictly non-refundable.
  • In the event of an inadvertent technical failure where a mock test cannot be initiated due to system errors, our technical team will reset your attempt or extend subscription validity at no charge.

4. Accidental Duplicate Transactions

If an applicant is debited twice for the same transaction due to a payment gateway timeout or internet connectivity glitch, 100% of the duplicate amount will be refunded to the original payment source immediately upon verification.

5. Refund Mode & Processing Timelines

All approved refunds are credited back to the original source instrument (Bank Account, UPI ID, or Credit/Debit Card) via our authorized payment gateway partner (Cashfree) within 5 to 7 business working days from the date of refund approval.

6. How to Apply for a Refund

To request a refund, submit a formal request with your Admission Receipt / Transaction ID, mobile number, and reason for withdrawal to:
