24-Hour Study Library Sanctuary Architecture
149 Seats • 2 Floors • Wi-Fi & Lockers
Step 1: Explore & Branch
Shift & Branch Choice
Aspirant explores facility features and selects study shift.
  • Morning, Afternoon, Night part-shifts
  • Full Day (7 AM - 9 PM) = ₹1,000/mo
  • 24-Hour Day & Night = ₹2,000/mo
  • Branch Tab for local branch admissions
Step 2: Seat & Locker Rule
149 Seat Allocation
Seat allocated across 2 dedicated study sanctuary floors.
  • 2nd Floor Zone A: Seats S-1 to S-24
  • 1st Floor Zone B, C, D: Seats S-25 to S-149
  • Seat No = Locker No (attached above seat)
  • Locker available for Full Shift only

Admission & Date Lock
Aspirant completes admission with non-alterable filing date.
  • Admission Date = Locked to form filing date
  • Fee = Shift Fee + ₹200 one-time registration
  • Dual mode: Online 3D Grid OR Front Desk
  • Instant C2SLIB-XXXXX membership ID
Step 4: Access Sanctuary
Wi-Fi & Member Portal
Member logs in with OTP to access Wi-Fi passwords and card.
  • 1st Floor: C2S-1 & C2S-2 (Protected)
  • 2nd Floor: C2S_Wifi_5G (Protected)
  • Digital Pass with live validity counter
  • Viral Referral Engine: Refer 1 = +7 Days Free
