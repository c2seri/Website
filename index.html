Exam Details — Posts, Eligibility, Pattern & Syllabus

Full details for all 10 exam tracks. Open any exam to read its posts, eligibility, pattern, syllabus overview and state relation. No exam dates or fees are invented here.

BANK Exams All India — Clerk, Probationary Officer (PO), Specialist Officer

Posts: Clerk, Probationary Officer (PO), Specialist Officer

Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline (exact age/qualification as per the official notification for that post).

Pattern: Prelims (objective) → Mains (objective + descriptive where applicable) → Interview / Language Proficiency Test, as per the specific bank exam.

Syllabus overview: Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning Ability, English Language, General/Financial Awareness, Computer Aptitude.

State relation: All-India recruitment through national banks and IBPS; state-wise vacancies are notified in the official advertisement.

Dates, fees and vacancy numbers are not listed here — check the official notification for that exam.

SSC Exams All India — CGL, CHSL, GD Constable, MTS and other Group B/C posts

Posts: CGL, CHSL, GD Constable, MTS and other Group B/C posts

Eligibility: 10th / 12th / Graduation depending on the post — check the official notification for that exam.

Pattern: Tier-based objective tests (and skill/descriptive tests where applicable), followed by document verification.

Syllabus overview: General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension.

State relation: All-India recruitment; regional vacancies and cut-offs vary by region — see the official notification.

Dates, fees and vacancy numbers are not listed here — check the official notification for that exam.

Railway Exams All India — NTPC, ALP, Technician, Group D and other RRB posts

Posts: NTPC, ALP, Technician, Group D and other RRB posts

Eligibility: 10th / ITI / 12th / Graduation depending on the post — check the official RRB notification.

Pattern: CBT Stage 1 → CBT Stage 2 (where applicable) → Aptitude / Typing / Physical test → Document verification and medical.

Syllabus overview: Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, General Science.

State relation: All-India recruitment through Railway Recruitment Boards; zones/regions are notified in the advertisement.

Dates, fees and vacancy numbers are not listed here — check the official notification for that exam.

UPSC Exams All India — IAS, IPS, IFS and other All-India / Central Services

Posts: IAS, IPS, IFS and other All-India / Central Services

Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline; age and attempt limits as per the official UPSC notification.

Pattern: Prelims (objective) → Mains (written) → Personality Test (interview).

Syllabus overview: General Studies, CSAT, Essay, Optional subject, Current Affairs.

State relation: All-India services; cadre allocation is state-related and notified by the government.

Dates, fees and vacancy numbers are not listed here — check the official notification for that exam.

Defence Exams All India — NDA, CDS, AFCAT and other officer entries

Posts: NDA, CDS, AFCAT and other officer entries

Eligibility: 12th (NDA) / Graduation (CDS, AFCAT) with subject conditions as per the official notification.

Pattern: Written test → SSB interview → Medical examination.

Syllabus overview: Mathematics, General Ability, English, General Knowledge.

State relation: All-India recruitment; no state quota in the written stage — see the official notification.

Dates, fees and vacancy numbers are not listed here — check the official notification for that exam.

Teaching Exams All India — TGT, PGT, PRT and other teaching posts

Posts: TGT, PGT, PRT and other teaching posts

Eligibility: Graduation / Post-graduation plus B.Ed. and TET/CTET qualification where required — check the official notification.

Pattern: Written test (subject + pedagogy + general) → Interview / Demo, where applicable.

Syllabus overview: Subject knowledge, Child Development & Pedagogy, General Awareness, Language.

State relation: Central and state teaching recruitments exist; this track covers the All-India pattern — state TETs are separate.

Dates, fees and vacancy numbers are not listed here — check the official notification for that exam.

OSSC Exams Odisha State — Various Group B/C posts under the Government of Odisha

Posts: Various Group B/C posts under the Government of Odisha

Eligibility: As per the OSSC notification for each post (qualification and age vary by post).

Pattern: Prelims → Mains → Skill / Document verification, where applicable.

Syllabus overview: General Awareness (with Odisha focus), Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, English, Odia where applicable.

State relation: Odisha state recruitment — OSSC, Government of Odisha.

Dates, fees and vacancy numbers are not listed here — check the official notification for that exam.

OSSSC Exams Odisha State — Various subordinate Group C/D posts under the Government of Odisha

Posts: Various subordinate Group C/D posts under the Government of Odisha

Eligibility: As per the OSSSC notification for each post (qualification and age vary by post).

Pattern: Written test → Skill / Physical test where applicable → Document verification.

Syllabus overview: General Awareness (with Odisha focus), Reasoning, Arithmetic, English, Odia where applicable.

State relation: Odisha state recruitment — OSSSC, Government of Odisha.

Dates, fees and vacancy numbers are not listed here — check the official notification for that exam.

OPSC Exams Odisha State — Odisha Administrative Service (OAS) and other Group A/B posts

Posts: Odisha Administrative Service (OAS) and other Group A/B posts

Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline; age and attempt limits as per the official OPSC notification.

Pattern: Prelims → Mains (written) → Personality Test (interview).

Syllabus overview: General Studies, Odia/English, Essay, Optional subject, Current Affairs with Odisha focus.

State relation: Odisha state recruitment — OPSC, Government of Odisha.

Dates, fees and vacancy numbers are not listed here — check the official notification for that exam.

Odisha Police Exams Odisha State — Constable, Sub-Inspector and other Odisha Police posts

Posts: Constable, Sub-Inspector and other Odisha Police posts

Eligibility: 10th / 12th / Graduation depending on the post, plus physical standards as per the official notification.

Pattern: Written test → Physical Measurement / Efficiency Test → Document verification and medical.

Syllabus overview: General Awareness (with Odisha focus), Reasoning, Arithmetic, English, Odia where applicable.

State relation: Odisha state recruitment — Odisha Police.
